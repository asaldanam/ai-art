CHARACTER REFERENCE — holly_willhelm / base / approved revision 1 / head_three_quarter

CANONICAL CHARACTER IDENTITY

Name and defining identity:
Holly Willhelm, an adult-presenting female Forsaken undead.

Species and physical presentation:
She has the intact appearance of a recently deceased person. Her exposed face
and neck are extremely pale cool ivory with a subtle gray-green undertone.
Her flesh is preserved rather than putrid. There are no visible open wounds,
rotting patches or exposed bones. Her hands and feet share the same very pale
cool ivory skin family with a subtle gray-green undertone. Each hand has five
intact digits, including one thumb, and each foot has five intact toes. Her
hands are slender and bony with long, dark, pointed nails. No digits are missing.

Build, proportions and recognizable silhouette:
She has a slim frame, a relatively narrow waist and an elongated neck.
Preserve the head-to-shoulder proportions, facial spacing and upper-torso
relationships of the selected Holly reference. Preserve the full-body
proportions documented by the original front, profile and back references.

Face structure and distinctive features:
Her face is narrow and oval, with defined cheekbones, subtly hollow cheeks,
a narrow jaw and a tapered chin. Her nose is fine and mostly straight.
Her lips are shaped but restrained, with a muted taupe-rose intrinsic color.
Her eyebrows are thin, dark and arched.

Eyes:
Her eyes are contained in size and almond-shaped, with defined lids, muted
gray-green orbital shadows and luminous yellow interiors. There are no
clearly defined pupils. Preserve their shape and spacing.

Hair:
Her hair is dark plum/burgundy, cut into a short layered bob with a center
part. The front locks fall on both sides of the face, tapering into pointed
ends near the base of the neck. The back is shorter at the nape. Preserve
the selected reference's hairline, center part, major lock arrangement and
overall hair silhouette.

Marks and anatomical asymmetries:
No distinctive facial scars, tattoos or jewelry are documented. Do not
invent permanent marks, missing facial tissue or new left/right asymmetries.
The ears remain covered by the established hairstyle.

Non-negotiable identifying features:
Very pale intact undead flesh, the narrow facial structure, luminous yellow
almond-shaped eyes, dark arched eyebrows and the center-parted short plum
bob must remain recognizable together. Do not replace her with a different
face that merely shares the same species or colors.

OUTFIT — base

Garment layers, shape and intrinsic colors:
Holly wears a floor-length robe with a fitted charcoal-purple upper body,
long sleeves and a flared lower silhouette. A dark charcoal under-skirt
falls in long vertical folds. The upper skirt is deep purple, with
silver-white borders and layered panels over the darker lower skirt.

Front construction:
The neckline forms a V bordered by branching silver-white ornamental trim.
The chest has symmetrical muted gray-green panels, curved rib-like
silver-gray framing and narrow subdued antique-gold borders. A distinctive
vertical silver-gray ornament follows the center of the torso. Preserve
the shapes and locations established by the selected midshot.

Shoulders and sleeves:
The shoulders are rounded cloth forms rather than oversized armor
pauldrons. Silver-white bands cross the upper-sleeve area. Dark olive
upper-arm panels have narrow subdued antique-gold edging. The long
charcoal-purple sleeves broaden toward the wrists and end in wide,
silver-white flared cuffs.

Waist and lower front:
A wide muted beige-gold belt wraps around the narrow waist. Small
silver-gray side fastenings remain on both sides as shown in the selected
reference. Silver-white borders continue down the purple upper skirt,
framing long pointed or curved panel ends over the charcoal under-skirt.
The floor-length hem is broad silver-white with narrow dark parallel
bands above it.

Side and back construction:
Preserve the original profile's fitted torso, sleeve flare and layered
robe depth. At the back, the short nape leaves part of the pale neck visible.
The dark shoulder yoke has silver-white edging and a broad pale band
across the shoulder-blade area. Beneath it, a fitted dark back panel has
subdued antique-gold piping. The gold belt continues around the waist.
Rear purple skirt panels are framed by silver-white borders descending
from near the belt and curving outward. Preserve the horizontal silver
border above the lower charcoal skirt and the broad banded hem.

Materials and accessories:
The primary robe and sleeves are cloth. Keep the pale ornamental borders
and the chest framing distinct from the dark cloth, with restrained
silver-gray and antique-gold material colors. No new straps, jewelry,
runes, badges, armor plates or other accessories are introduced.
No staff or other weapon is carried for the base reference set. Her feet are
bare: only a small part of the forefeet and toes extends beyond the robe hem.
No boots, shoes, sandals or other footwear are added.

Outfit-specific invariants:
Preserve the V neckline, branching collar motif, symmetrical chest
construction, central vertical ornament, shoulder bands, flared pale
cuffs, wide gold belt and silver-edged skirt layers in their established
positions. Do not swap anatomical sides or add permanent costume damage.
